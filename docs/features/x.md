# X (Twitter)

Applies on `twitter.com`, `x.com` and their subdomains.

- Everything in [All sites](all-sites.md):
  - pages can't switch on looping;
  - videos pause at the end and don't replay by themselves;
  - a video within 0.3 s of its end counts as finished.
- Nothing else. No autoplay blocking, no loop button.
